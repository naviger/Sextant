# Logo Usage

## Summary

The brand uses a company logo and a product logo, with a strict hierarchy that preserves brand clarity and product recognition. The company logo is used only when company branding is explicitly required, while the product logo appears in the app and general product contexts.

## Key Rules

- Use the product logo in product interfaces and primary brand surfaces.
- Use the company logo only in explicit company contexts such as splash screens or about screens.
- When the company and product logos appear together, the company mark precedes the product mark.
- Keep logo usage consistent and restrained.

## Allowed Patterns

- Product logo used in app headers and product surfaces.
- Company logo used only in brand or about contexts.
- Vertical or horizontal company logo treatments used appropriately.
- Product logo positioned consistently in the UI.

## Forbidden Patterns

- Replacing the product logo with the company logo in product interfaces.
- Using lettermark or decorative alternatives in place of approved logos.
- Mixing product and company branding without hierarchy.
- Using logos in low-contrast or chaotic layouts.
