# Motion Design

## Summary

Motion should feel oceanic, smooth, and deliberate. It reinforces the navigation metaphor without becoming distracting or gamified.

## Key Rules

- Prefer smooth and continuous transitions.
- Use motion to suggest directional flow and signal changes.
- Keep transitions predictable and elegant.
- Use motion as a subtle instrument, not an attention-grabbing gimmick.

## Allowed Patterns

- Current-like flow animations.
- Constellation expansion transitions.
- Bearing transitions and directional shifts.
- Course tracing and smooth chart updates.
- Deliberate, oceanic movement language.

## Forbidden Patterns

- Bounce effects.
- Flashing animations.
- Sudden visual jumps.
- Mechanical or gamified motion.
- Lengthy animation sequences that reduce clarity.
