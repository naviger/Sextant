# AI Experience Language

## Summary

The product intentionally replaces generic AI language with navigation terminology. AI is framed as guidance, directional assistance, and strategic instrumentation rather than as a generic chatbot or automation layer.

## Key Rules

- Use navigation terms instead of generic AI terms.
- Keep AI outputs explainable, source-aware, and confidence-based.
- Frame AI suggestions as bearings, fixes, and corrections.
- Make AI feel like a strategic instrument rather than a conversational gimmick.

## Allowed Patterns

- Bearings = suggested directions.
- Position Fix = current strategic position.
- Course Correction = proposed alignment adjustment.
- Signals = detected risks or opportunities.
- Currents = trends or cross-organizational forces.
- North Star = ultimate objective.
- Drift = loss of strategic alignment.

## Forbidden Patterns

- Chatbot-first AI language or generic assistant style.
- Vague “insight” vocabulary without context or source.
- Overstating certainty or automating business decisions without explanation.
- AI messaging that ignores the navigation metaphor or undermines executive trust.
