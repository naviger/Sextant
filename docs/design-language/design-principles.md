# Design Principles

## Summary

The design principles define the general behavior of the product experience. They ensure that the interface feels precise, trustworthy, and executive-ready, while making strategic complexity visible without becoming noisy.

## Key Rules

- Calm over excitement.
- Strategic depth over surface simplicity.
- Alignment visibility over decorative storytelling.
- Progressive disclosure over information overload.
- Trust, precision, and sophistication over flashy interaction.

## Allowed Patterns

- Spacious layouts and strong information hierarchy.
- Strong difference between primary and secondary emphasis.
- Multi-layer strategic context with progressive reveal.
- High information density without clutter.
- Clear separation between strategic signal and tactical noise.

## Forbidden Patterns

- Neon aesthetics.
- Excessive motion and visual noise.
- Cyberpunk or gimmicky AI design language.
- Overloading the screen with equal-weight content.
- Decorative presentation that hides insight quality.
