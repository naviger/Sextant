# Brand Vision

## Summary

Naviger Sextant helps complex organizations chart, align, and continuously adjust strategy across the enterprise. The brand vision is not about project execution or tactical task management; it is about strategic navigation, clarity of direction, and trusted organizational alignment.

## Key Rules

- Position the product as a system of navigation, not a system of record.
- Use language that conveys strategic clarity and executive confidence.
- Keep the experience calm, measured, and trustworthy.
- Frame the product as supporting alignment across the entire organization.
- Emphasize clarity of current position, target direction, and route adjustment.

## Allowed Patterns

- “Where are we now, where are we trying to go, and how do we get there?”
- Executive-grade strategic language.
- Navigation-oriented metaphors tied to enterprise decision-making.
- Calm visual density and clarity.
- Strategic alignment presented as a connected system instead of isolated artifacts.

## Forbidden Patterns

- Generic PM or task management language.
- Neon or overly playful visual messaging.
- “AI toy” vibes or hype-driven design language.
- Pirate, ship-wheel, or literal maritime cliché imagery.
- Tactical execution framing that hides the strategic purpose.
