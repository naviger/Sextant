# Data Visualization Language

## Summary

The product uses charting conventions inspired by navigation instruments, strategic currents, and celestial maps. Data should communicate confidence, direction, risk, and organizational flow rather than simple business KPI output.

## Key Rules

- Use layered and directional visualizations rather than flat metrics-only views.
- Express strategic depth using contour-like indicators and layered bands.
- Use a map or chart perspective to show relationships and alignment rather than disconnected cards.
- Treat deviation as drift, not generic negativity.

## Allowed Patterns

- Strategic currents shown as flowing directional lines.
- Alignment maps with enterprise strategy at the center.
- Constellation-like relationship views around a central North Star.
- Contour bands and layered indicators for confidence or readiness.
- Course deviation states such as On Course, Minor Drift, Significant Drift, Off Bearing.

## Forbidden Patterns

- Simple isolated KPI tiles with no contextual relationship.
- Overly literal or decorative charting that distracts from the strategic narrative.
- Status messaging that ignores the navigation metaphor.
- Treating drift as a generic error rather than directional loss.
