# Typography

## Summary

Typography is used to communicate precision, confidence, and executive clarity. The system balances modern technical elegance with readability across dense strategic dashboards and lighter narrative surfaces.

## Key Rules

- Use modern, technical, executive-grade fonts.
- Keep headings strong, precise, and highly readable.
- Reserve mono-space typography for metrics, instrument-like data, and quantitative measurement.
- Maintain hierarchy and clarity across strategic dashboards and chart surfaces.

## Allowed Patterns

- IBM Plex Sans or Inter Tight for headings.
- Inter for body copy.
- IBM Plex Mono for metrics, coordinates, scores, and KPIs.
- Established hierarchy between titles, labels, and data.

## Forbidden Patterns

- Decorative or novelty typography.
- Overuse of condensed styles or display fonts in dense executive content.
- Inconsistent hierarchy across dashboards or views.
- Typography that sacrifices readability for visual flair.
