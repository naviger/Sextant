# Experience Principles

## Summary

The experience principles define what each interaction should communicate to the user. The goal is to make the organization’s strategic reality legible, directional, and aligned at every step.

## Key Rules

- Know where you are.
- Know where you are going.
- Know what is moving you.
- Know what is holding you back.
- Keep the fleet aligned.

## Allowed Patterns

- Current-state context with clear directional outcomes.
- Strategic relationship mapping.
- Confidence and uncertainty indicators.
- Risk and drift visibility in context.
- Executive-friendly, decision-oriented surfaces.

## Forbidden Patterns

- Interfaces that hide uncertainty or context.
- Navigation that loses the user’s strategic position.
- Surfaces that present noise as signal.
- Disconnected business views without alignment context.
