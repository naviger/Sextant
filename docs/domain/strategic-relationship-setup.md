# Strategic Relationship Setup

## Intent

Strategic relationships define participation links between organization nodes.

They do not define strategy content.

## Topology

Relationships may:
- Follow hierarchy
- Skip hierarchy levels
- Connect peers
- Connect functions
- Connect regions
- Connect centers of excellence

## Fields

- Relationship Type: Governance, Collaboration, Dependency, Oversight
- Participation Role: Lead, Required Participant, Contributor, Observer
- Strategy Role: Defines, Co-Defines, Responds, Executes
- Review Cadence: Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, Annual
- Status: Draft, Active, Suspended, Retired
- Notes: long-form rationale for why the relationship exists

## Example rationale

Corporate has direct oversight due to strategic market importance.

## Boundary

Strategic relationship setup does not include strategy execution content:

- Drivers
- Objectives
- Strategic Choices
- Capabilities
- Initiatives
- Outcomes
- Planning Cycles

Future integration rule:

- Relationships define who participates.
- For Planning Cycles, active relationships influence suggested participants only.
- Suggested participants are optional and do not automatically create participation records.
