# Bearing

## Definition

A bearing is the current directional guidance or interpretation of how well the organization is trending relative to the target state. It represents a direction of travel rather than a single point outcome.

## Business Purpose

The system is intended to give leaders “bearings” for strategic guidance and AI-assisted recommendations. Bearings help translate strategic context into actionable directional cues.

## Relationships

- A bearing is informed by signals and current position data.
- It supports the interpretation of strategic health or confidence.
- It can vary by strategy, business unit, or initiative.
- It relates to both course correction and alignment assessment.
