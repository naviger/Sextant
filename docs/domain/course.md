# Course

## Definition

A course is the active path or execution plan an organization or business unit is following toward a strategic objective or North Star.

## Business Purpose

The platform helps teams manage and compare courses by business unit, initiative, and strategic theme. Courses are the operational representation of strategic intent.

## Relationships

- A course is adopted by a strategy or business unit.
- It is influenced by current conditions, signals, and drift.
- It may require adjustment when recommendations or position fixes indicate misalignment.
- It describes the path to the intended outcome.
