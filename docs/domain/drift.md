# Drift

## Definition

Drift is the measurable divergence between the current strategic course and the desired direction. It represents the gap between intent and current reality.

## Business Purpose

The product specifically emphasizes “detect strategic drift.” Drift is one of the central decision-support concepts because it highlights where the organization is moving off course and where intervention is needed.

## Relationships

- Drift is detected between the current position fix and the intended strategy or North Star.
- Drift can be observed at the objective, initiative, or business-unit level.
- Drift drives recommended corrective actions.
- Drift is a leading indicator for alignment risk and strategic misalignment.
