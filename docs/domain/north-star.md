# NorthStar

## Definition

The North Star is the enterprise vision or guiding direction that defines the overarching purpose of the organization. It acts as the central reference point for strategy alignment and navigation.

## Business Purpose

The product is specifically framed around navigation and direction. The North Star gives leaders and teams a single reference point to compare their current course, strategic themes, and business-unit actions.

## Relationships

- A North Star informs and constrains the strategy.
- Business-unit strategies and objectives align to the North Star.
- Drift is measured as deviation from the North Star.
- It is the anchor used in position-fix and confidence assessments.
