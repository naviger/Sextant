# Naviger Sextant Design Language
## Charting Strategic Direction

### Brand Vision

Naviger Sextant helps complex organizations chart, align, and continuously adjust strategy across the enterprise. By combining AI guidance with a navigation-first operating model, leaders gain a clear fix on their current position, a shared North Star, and confidence that every business unit is sailing on the same course.

---

# Core Brand Idea

## Strategic Navigation

Naviger Sextant is not a system of record.

It is a system of navigation.

The platform helps leaders answer:

> Where are we now, where are we trying to go, and what is the best path to get there while keeping the organization aligned?

The primary metaphor is not a ship.

The primary metaphor is a **navigation bridge** equipped with the instruments needed to guide an entire fleet.

---

# Design Principles

## 1. Calm Over Excitement

Executives need confidence, not spectacle.

The experience should feel:

- Intentional
- Measured
- Sophisticated
- Intelligent
- Trustworthy

### Design Characteristics

- Spacious layouts
- Strong information hierarchy
- Limited accent colors
- Purposeful motion
- High information density without clutter

### Avoid

- Neon aesthetics
- Excessive animations
- "AI toy" experiences
- Cyberpunk visuals
- Visual noise

---

## 2. Strategic Depth

Every screen should suggest that deeper insights exist beneath the surface.

### Inspiration

Like a nautical chart showing ocean depth contours.

### Interface Expression

- Multi-layer visualizations
- Expandable strategic context
- Visible strategic dependencies
- Confidence and uncertainty indicators
- Progressive disclosure

---

## 3. Alignment Visibility

Alignment is the product's core value.

Every experience should reinforce:

- Relationships
- Dependencies
- Strategic inheritance
- Enterprise-to-business-unit connections
- Cause-and-effect pathways

### User Feeling

> "I can see how everything connects."

# Visual Metaphors

## Primary Metaphor: Nautical Charts

Avoid literal maritime clichés.

### Use

- Bathymetric contour lines
- Navigation grids
- Latitude/longitude references
- Bearings
- Courses
- Ocean currents
- Navigation markers

### Do Not Use

- Anchors
- Pirate imagery
- Ship wheels
- Sailboat illustrations
- Nautical clip art

---

## Secondary Metaphor: Celestial Navigation

A sextant determines position through relationships.

This provides a powerful analogy for strategy.

| Navigation | Strategy |
|------------|------------|
| North Star | Enterprise Vision |
| Constellations | Strategic Themes |
| Stars | Objectives |
| Bearings | AI Guidance |
| Courses | Execution Paths |
| Current Position Fix | Organizational Reality |
| Drift | Misalignment |

# Data Visualization Language

## Strategic Currents

Representation of influence and movement.

### Use For

- Dependencies
- Strategic momentum
- Organizational influence
- Risk propagation

### Visual Expression

Flowing directional lines rather than simple arrows.

---

## Alignment Maps

Visualization of strategic alignment.

### Structure

- Enterprise strategy at center
- Strategic themes orbit around vision
- Business-unit strategies connect outward
- Dependencies appear as currents

### Inspiration

Constellations surrounding a North Star.

---

## Strategic Depth Indicators

Inspired by nautical depth contours.

### Use For

- Confidence
- Maturity
- Risk
- Complexity
- Strategic readiness

### Expression

Layered contour bands instead of simple percentages.

---

## Course Deviation

Misalignment should be framed as navigation drift.

### Status Language

| Status | Meaning |
|----------|----------|
| On Course | Fully aligned |
| Minor Drift | Emerging concerns |
| Significant Drift | Material misalignment |
| Off Bearing | Strategic conflict |

# Motion Design

Motion should feel oceanic and deliberate.

## Characteristics

- Smooth
- Continuous
- Predictable
- Elegant

### Inspiration

Ocean currents rather than mechanical motion.

### Examples

- Current flows
- Constellation expansion
- Bearing transitions
- Course tracing

### Avoid

- Bounce effects
- Flashing animations
- Abrupt transitions
- Gamified motion
